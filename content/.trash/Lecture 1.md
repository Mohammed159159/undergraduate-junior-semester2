> [!Note] [15:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RMINw5r4lVU&t=915#t=15:14.64) [[Material Strength]] vs. [[Applied Stress]]
> ![[Pasted image 20250217151924.png]]
> - $\large \sigma_{\text{UTS}}$ and $\large \sigma_{\text{y}}$ are constant regardless of the **dimensions** and **load**
> - However, stress varies with strain
> - For a given material, *strength* is a property of this material, but *stress* applied on a part made of this material depends on the dimensions and load applied on this part
