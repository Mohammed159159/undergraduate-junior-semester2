> [!Note] [40:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADRx8PfSvSQ&t=2422#t=40:21.55) [[Slider-crank mechanism]]
> > [!tip] $L=4$ and $P_l = 4$ OR $L=3$, $P_l = 2$ and $P_h = 1$ are the minimum conditions for a mechanism
> 
> - Converts linear motion of piston to rotational motion at crank (reciprocating engine) or vise versa (reciprocating compressor)
> - Rotational motion is more favorable to deal with due to its continuous nature
>   [[Kinematic Diagram]] vs. [[Construction Drawing]]
> ![[Pasted image 20250222150259.png]]
> - A [[02 Mechanics of Machines/Mechanisms|Mechanism]] is characterized by the number of pairs and number of links
> - [[Inversion of a Mechanism]] involves implementing changes in the mechanism (i.e. changing the fixed link) to get a different variation of the mechanism, without changing the number of links or pairs

> [!Note] [01:25:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADRx8PfSvSQ&t=5141#t=1:25:40.70) [[Double slider mechanism]] > [[Ellipse trammels]]
> ![[Pasted image 20250222150557.png]]
> - Achieves ellipse equation by relating $L\cos\theta, L\sin\theta, x$, and $y$ using the fact that $x^2 + y^2 = L^2$
>   ![[Pasted image 20250222151039.png]]
>   ![[Pasted image 20250222151156.png]]
> > [!tip] This mechanism is a great example of how mechanisms are used for satisfying mathematical equations (e.g. mechanical computers)
> > - Any mechanism is characterized by inputs and outputs in terms of mathematical relations

> > [!info] There are three [[Types of motion]]
