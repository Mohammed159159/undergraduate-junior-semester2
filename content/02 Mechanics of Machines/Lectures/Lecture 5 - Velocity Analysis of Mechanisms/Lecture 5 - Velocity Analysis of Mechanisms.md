> [!Note] [01:45:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fTj4tjo26IM&t=6313#t=1:45:13.19) [[Relative velocity of two points on of a moving link]]
> - Rigid link in rotation => Constant magnitude of position vector
> - Elastic link OR Rigid link in translation => Variable magnitude of position vector
>   
>   
> $$\Huge \frac{d}{dt}\vec{R}=R\omega \angle{(\theta + 90^\circ)} + \dot{R}\angle{\theta}$$

> [!Note] [01:54:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fTj4tjo26IM&t=6894#t=1:54:54.42) Summary of [[Relative velocity of two points on of a moving link]]
> - Consider two points with a position vector between them
> - That position vector could change in terms of
> 	- Magnitude (getting the points closer or further away from each other)
> 	- Direction (rotating one point with respect to the other)
> - The relative velocity (change of that position vector with respect to time) between these two points has two components
> 	- In the same direction as the position vector with value corresponding to the rate of change of the magnitude of the position vector
> 	- Perpendicular to the position vector with value corresponding to the rate of change of the direction of the position vector
