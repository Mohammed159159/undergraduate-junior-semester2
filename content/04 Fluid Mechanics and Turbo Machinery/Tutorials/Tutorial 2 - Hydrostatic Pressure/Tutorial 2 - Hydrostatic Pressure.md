> Pressure at one end =
> - Pressures carried by this pressure $+P$
> - Heights carried by the pressure => $+\rho gh$
> - Pressures helping this pressure => $-P$
> - Heights helping the pressure => $-\rho gh$
>

> > - Also similar to finding finding net force; force = opposite forces - same direction forces
