> [!Note] [38:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8bOouR5n7jc&t=2308#t=38:27.52) [[Network Diagram]]
> Visualize dependencies between tasks in the project
> ![[Pasted image 20250221101554.png]]
> There are two methods to draw a network diagram
> ![[Pasted image 20250221101711.png]]
> ![[Pasted image 20250221103457.png]]
> 1. [[Activity on Arrow]]
> 	- Nodes are the start and end of activities
> 	- Arrows represent which activity or activities start after the end node 
> 2. [[Activity on Node]]
> 	- Nodes are the activities
> 	- Arrows represent that a certain portion of the activity or activities at the tail of the arrow needs to be completed before the start of the activity at the head of the arrow
> 	  
> > [!important] Dependencies are stated in terms of the immediate predecessors 
> 
> > [!question] Why can't I have two activities starting at one shared node and ending at another shared node?
> > AOA networks rely on unique arrows to define the sequence of activities and dependencies. If two arrows have the same start and end node, it implies redundant or conflicting dependencies.
